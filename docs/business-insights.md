# Executive Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations

**Project:** Customer Retention & Revenue Intelligence Platform  
**Evidence Base:** 99,441 Orders across 95,560 Customers (2016–2018)  

---

## 1. Key Business Findings

### 1.1 Customer Retention & Repeat Purchasing Dynamics
- **Single-Transaction Dominance:** **96.88%** of customers (92,570 customers) placed only a single order. Only **3.12%** (2,990 customers) are repeat purchasers.
- **High Monetary Disparity:** Despite representing 3.12% of customers, repeat shoppers generate **6.1% of platform revenue (R$ 967K)**.
- **Spend Multiplier:** The average repeat customer spends **R$ 323.41** compared to **R$ 157.80** for a one-time customer—a **2.05x spend multiplier**.
- **Cohort Decay:** Retention drops precipitously after Month 0. Month 1 retention across 2017 cohorts averages **0.4% – 0.7%**, indicating the absence of post-purchase engagement workflows.

### 1.2 Customer Segmentation (RFM Insights)
- **Champions & Loyalists:** 3,892 customers represent the core high-value advocates who purchase across multiple periods with high order values.
- **Hibernating High Spenders:** Over 18,000 customers spent substantial sums (R$ 300+) on single purchases 6–12 months ago but have never returned. They represent the single highest ROI re-engagement target.
- **Lost / Churned Customers:** 32.5% of the database consists of low-recency, low-monetary one-off buyers.

### 1.3 Logistics SLA & Customer Satisfaction Correlation
- **On-Time Delivery SLA:** Overall platform on-time delivery rate is **92.1%** with an average delivery duration of **12.5 days**.
- **Customer Rating Cliff:** 
  - On-time deliveries achieve an average review score of **4.23 / 5.0** (76% 5-star ratings).
  - Late deliveries drop to **2.45 / 5.0** (53% 1-star ratings).
  - Delivery delay represents the **single highest driver of negative customer reviews**.
- **Regional Disparities:** Southeastern states (SP, PR, MG) enjoy rapid 8–11 day delivery times with late rates under 6%. Northern and northeastern states (AL, MA, PA, RR) face 22–28 day transit times with late rates exceeding 18%.

### 1.4 Category & Seller Concentration
- **Pareto Rule:** 15 out of 74 categories generate 70% of total GMV.
- **Top Grossing Categories:**
  1. `bed_bath_table`: R$ 1.04M (6.5% share)
  2. `health_beauty`: R$ 1.26M (7.9% share)
  3. `watches_gifts`: R$ 1.21M (7.6% share)
  4. `sports_leisure`: R$ 0.99M (6.2% share)
  5. `computers_accessories`: R$ 0.91M (5.7% share)

## 2. Strategic Recommendations for Growth

1. **Deploy Post-Purchase Re-Engagement Automations:** Implement triggered replenishment emails 30, 60, and 90 days after delivery for consumable categories (Health/Beauty, Pet Shop).
2. **Launch a Tiered Customer Loyalty Program:** Provide free freight credits or discount vouchers for 2nd orders to double the repeat purchase rate from 3.1% to 6.2%, unlocking an estimated R$ 1.2M in annual GMV.
3. **Establish Regional Fulfillment Hubs:** Partner with logistics 3PLs in northeastern Brazil to cut transit times from 25 days to under 14 days, protecting review scores.
4. **Target Hibernating High Spenders:** Run dedicated remarketing campaigns targeting the 18,000 customers who spent over R$ 300 in the past.
